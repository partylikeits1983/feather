\maketitle
Let $\mathbb{F}$ be a field. For a polynomial
\[
f(X) = \sum_{i=0}^{d} a_i X^i,
\]
evaluation at $n$ distinct points produces a Reed--Solomon codeword.
Two different polynomials agree at at most $d$ points, so the minimum distance is $n-d$.
